At the start of a new class, there is always excitement and apprehension. There is so much to learn, and so much to learn --and nowhere more than in an accelerated course!

Re-stating the obvious: There is a semester of work to do, and a shortened amount of time to do it.

Some students thrive in this environment. If you have ever been a procrastinator, you are familiar with the feeling of pressure that comes with a looming deadline. On the plus side, a short-term course is a sprint! You can focus your energy and time commitment to the course for a shortened period of time. Of course the other side of that coin is that you have to be very disciplined with your time management.

It's week one. You have a fresh start. I was very encouraged this morning to look and see that most of you have already logged into the course site, and started looking around. That is a great first step!

Also note that the shortened time frame also means that my required drop deadline is earlier than in a regular semester too. I am happy to see that you are hitting the ground running! There won't be time to procrastinate here.
